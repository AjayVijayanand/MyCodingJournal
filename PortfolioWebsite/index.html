  • Automatic volume-controlled audio amplifier

    NTU EE2073, AY2025/26

    A closed-loop audio amplifier on an STM32 with three subsystems: a voltage-controlled amplifier, a power amplifier and a VU meter. It holds a stable output across a wide input range. I validated a flat frequency response across the audible range, characterised the amplifier's gain across the full control-voltage range, and debugged clipping and grounding noise during integration.

    STM32, Python, Jupyter, closed-loop feedback

  • Three-stage analog stethoscope signal chain

    NTU EE2102, AY2025/26

    A signal chain that takes body sounds from a microphone to an earpiece. Stage one is an inverting op-amp preamplifier with a feedback capacitor to suppress high-frequency noise. Stage two is a log-scale VU meter that lights LEDs at rising peak voltages using diode threshold logic. Stage three is an emitter-follower buffer that supplies enough current to drive low-impedance earpieces.

    Op-amp and BJT circuits, LTspice, oscilloscope measurement

    A chat room and a small blog that run in the terminal and store everything in MongoDB, with accounts, hashed passwords and separate admin and reader roles.

    Python, pymongo, MongoDB
